Shrinking Milka chocolate bar tricked consumers, says German court
BBC World News • 13 May 2026, 11:29 PM NZST • Priority: 9/10
A German court has ruled that Mondelēz, the manufacturer of Milka's Alpine Milk chocolate bar, deceived consumers and broke competition law by reducing the bar's weight from 100g to 90g while maintaining similar packaging. This decision follows a lawsuit brought by Hamburg's consumer protection office (VZHH) that accused the company of "shrinkflation".
Why this matters: This case highlights the increasing scrutiny of "shrinkflation" tactics by consumer protection agencies and courts, potentially setting a precedent for other similar cases against manufacturers reducing product sizes without clearly indicating the change to consumers. Businesses should take note and consider the long-term reputational risks associated with these practices, especially as consumers become more aware and vocal about them. This also reinforces the need for transparency in pricing and packaging to maintain consumer trust and avoid legal challenges.
