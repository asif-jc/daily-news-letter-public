Amsterdam bans public adverts for meat and fossil fuels
BBC World News • 04 May 2026, 11:07 AM NZST • Priority: 8/10
Amsterdam has become the first capital city globally to ban public advertisements for meat and fossil fuel products, a decision spearheaded by politicians seeking to align the city's appearance with its environmental goals, including becoming carbon neutral by 2050. The bans, effective since May 1st, remove ads for products like burgers, petrol cars, and airlines from public spaces.
Why this matters: This move from Amsterdam signals a growing global trend towards prioritizing environmental concerns over traditional advertising revenue and consumer choice. It highlights the increasing pressure on businesses associated with high-carbon products and meat consumption to change their marketing strategies and potentially adjust their business models. Companies should pay attention to this trend as other cities might follow Amsterdam's lead, potentially impacting their advertising reach and brand perception.
